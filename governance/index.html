CASPER MANAGEMENT

Governance & Laws

CASPER is governed globally by its three-member Governance Body, while individual Sectors manage their own intra-Sector activity. Sectors operate freely within CASPER's laws and competition limits.

THE CASPER GOVERNANCE BODY

ArjunREPRESENTATIVE

The Representative member of the central CASPER Governance Body.

DECISION MAKING

General Decisions

Global matters are decided by the Founder, Neutral and Representative. A majority of the three is required.

Major CASPER Changes

Full rule changes, removing Sectors and other major structural changes are voted on by every CASPER Host together with the three Governance Body members. A majority is required.

No Appeals

CASPER Management fully checks the relevant information before acting. CASPER disciplinary decisions are final and cannot be appealed.

SECTOR GOVERNANCE

Sector Autonomy

Sectors manage their own intra-Sector activity. The central Governance Body does not interfere with ordinary local matters.

Sector Hosts

Every Sector has a Host responsible for local activity and Sector competitions. Hosts must operate within CASPER's global laws.

Global Authority

The central three govern CASPER globally. Sector Hosts participate in votes on major structural changes.

COMPETITION GOVERNANCE

Sport-Specific Competition Rules

Competitions are designed and managed by Sectors. Football and Cricket are freeform within their limits; Futsal follows stricter seasonal requirements.

SportMaximum Cups / SeasonSeason Structure
Futsal15Strict: 1 League + 1 Cup + 1 Championship required every season
Football5Freeform: no League/Cup/Championship requirement
Cricket4Freeform: no League/Cup/Championship requirement

Futsal: the seasonal structure is mandatory. Football and Cricket: Sectors may freely choose their competition structure, including running one or multiple cups, provided they stay within the applicable seasonal limits and CASPER laws.

CASPER DISCIPLINE

Yellow Cards

Yellow cards are recorded by CASPER Discipline. Accumulation may trigger a Black Card.

Red Cards

Automatic 1-match ban. In Futsal, the opponent receives +1 goal and the player starts the next match 1 goal down. In Football, the player serves the next-match ban.

Black Cards

Football: 3-match ban. Futsal: opponent receives +2 goals and the player starts each of the next 3 matches 1 goal down.

Black Card Triggers

  • 3 Yellow Cards within 3 matches.
  • 1 Red Card and 1 Yellow Card within 3 matches.
  • Direct Black Card for cheating.
  • Direct Black Card for brutal fouls.
MATCH OFFICIATING

Referee Authority

The referee officiates the match and applies the laws during play. CASPER Discipline manages the resulting disciplinary consequences under CASPER rules.

SPORT LAWS

Futsal

  • 1v1.
  • Standard match: 2 minutes.
  • Finals: 3 minutes.
  • Field size: 3 × 1.5 m to 7 × 5 m.

Football

  • 4v4 standard.
  • 5v5 maximum.
  • Field size: 12 × 6 m to 40 × 20 m.

Cricket

  • 1v1 or team-based.
  • Maximum team size: 4.
  • Playing area: 10 × 15 m to 50 × 30 m.
  • Boundaries required.
GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

Sectors have freedom until CASPER's laws say otherwise.

Local activity stays with the Sector Host. Global governance stays with the central three. Futsal follows strict seasonal competition requirements, while Football and Cricket remain freeform within their limits.
