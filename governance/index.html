CASPER MANAGEMENT

Governance & Laws

CASPER is governed centrally by three people, while individual Sectors manage their own intra-Sector activity. Sectors have freedom to run competitions and operate locally as long as they stay within CASPER's laws and competition limits.

The CASPER Governance Body

Founder

Anish

The Founder and one of the three members of the central CASPER Governance Body.

Neutral

Vyom

The Neutral member of the central Governance Body.

Representative

Arjun

The Representative member of the central Governance Body.

These three form the central CASPER Governance Body. Their decisions govern CASPER at the global level, and a majority is required for decisions made by the three.

Who Decides What

Intra-Sector matters

CASPER's central Governance Body does not interfere with ordinary activity inside a Sector. The Sector Host manages those matters.

Global matters

The Founder, Neutral and Representative make global CASPER decisions. A majority of the three is required.

Major CASPER changes

Major decisions, such as completely changing CASPER's rules or removing Sectors, are voted on by every CASPER Host together with the three members of the Governance Body. A majority is required.

Sector Hosts

Every Sector has a Host. The Host is responsible for running and managing the Sector's intra-Sector activity and its competitions.

  • Sectors can decide how they run their own local activity.
  • Sectors can create and manage competitions within CASPER's limits.
  • Sector decisions must remain within CASPER's global laws.
  • For major global changes, every Sector Host gets a vote alongside the central Governance Body.

Competition Governance

Competitions are designed and managed by Sectors, subject to CASPER's global restrictions.

The Championship must use a group stage followed by a knockout stage. After the required League, Cup and Championship are provided, a Sector may run additional competitions within the applicable seasonal limits.

CASPER Discipline

Yellow cards

Yellow cards are recorded by CASPER Discipline. Accumulation can trigger a Black Card under the rules below.

Red cards

A Red Card automatically gives a 1-match ban.

  • Football: the player serves the next-match ban.
  • Futsal: the opponent receives +1 goal, and the player starts the next match 1 goal down.

Black cards

A Black Card is a serious disciplinary sanction.

  • Football: 3-match ban.
  • Futsal: opponent receives +2 goals, and the player starts each of the next 3 matches 1 goal down.

When a Black Card applies

  • 3 Yellow Cards within 3 matches.
  • 1 Red Card and 1 Yellow Card within 3 matches.
  • A direct Black Card awarded for cheating.
  • A direct Black Card awarded for brutal fouls.
There are no appeals. CASPER Management fully checks the relevant information before a disciplinary action is issued.

Match Officiating

The referee officiates matches. Match officials are responsible for applying the laws during the match, while CASPER Discipline handles disciplinary consequences under the CASPER rules.

Sport Laws

Futsal

  • 1v1.
  • Standard match: 2 minutes.
  • Finals: 3 minutes.
  • Field size may range from 3 × 1.5 m to 7 × 5 m.
  • Red and Black Card goal handicaps apply as specified in CASPER Discipline.

Football

  • 4v4 is the standard format.
  • 5v5 is the maximum allowed format.
  • Field size may range from 12 × 6 m to 40 × 20 m.
  • Red Card: automatic 1-match ban.
  • Black Card: 3-match ban.

Cricket

  • May be 1v1 or team-based.
  • Maximum team size: 4 players.
  • Playing area may range from 10 × 15 m to 50 × 30 m.
  • Boundaries are required.

Core Governance Principle

Sectors have freedom until CASPER's laws say otherwise. Local activity stays with the Sector Host. Global governance stays with the central three. Major structural changes require the wider CASPER Host vote.
