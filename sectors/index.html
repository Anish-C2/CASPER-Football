Sectors

The local operating layer of CASPER. Players may register in two sectors maximum.

One CASPER. Many operating areas.

A Sector is a physical location where CASPER operates. It may be a school, apartment, community, city or another local area, and sectors can exist anywhere in the world. Every sector can run its own players, clubs, leagues, competitions and seasons while remaining part of CASPER.

SECTOR ≠ COUNTRY   •   SECTOR = LOCAL CASPER ECOSYSTEM   •   MAX 2 SECTORS PER PLAYER
